Features

Everything you need at the board.

Play friends on one screen, take on KataGo or any GTP engine, review your games, or take on the world on OGS.

  • ◎

    Live AI analysis

    KataGo runs in the background from the moment Hoshi opens and rates every move: best, excellent, good, inaccuracy, mistake or blunder, with points lost.

  • ▦

    Territory at a glance

    See secure and potential territory and the estimated score, from Hoshi's own estimator or KataGo's ownership map.

  • ▤

    Your game libraryNew

    Every game you play is kept. Open one later and step through it: KataGo judges each move again, with all its effects and music.

  • ❖

    SGF editor

    Open and save SGF files, explore variations in a game tree, add comments, marks and setup stones.

  • ⚑

    Pin what matteredNew

    Press S during any game and pin moments as a mistake, good move, question, key moment, joseki, life and death or lesson. A timeline shows them along the game.

  • ↗

    Draw your thinkingNew

    Arrows, areas, circles, letters and numbered "what if" lines, without touching the game. During your OGS games it is notes only: no engine, and drawing never sends a move.

  • ⇆

    Share and discussNew

    Save the study as an SGF and send it to a friend or a teacher. Their notes and replies merge into yours; other programs still read it as comments.

  • ?

    What would you play?New

    A quiz built from your pins, and a printable game report with every moment, the notes and KataGo's verdict. See a sample report

  • ◈

    Joseki on the boardNew

    Turn on Joseki and every corner shows the known continuations, coloured by how the OGS Joseki Explorer rates them, with its comments in the sidebar.

  • ❀

    Joseki trainerNew

    Practise lines on the main board in random corners. Spaced repetition brings back the ones you miss; fetch new lines from OGS or import your own SGF.

  • ⌁

    Online on OGS

    Sign in to online-go.com, accept challenges and play in real time with clock and chat. Hoshi only creates unranked games.

  • ●

    Face to face

    Two players, one screen, on 19×19, 13×13 or 9×9, with pass and undo — every move still judged by KataGo.

  • ⇄

    Yours, offline

    Free and open source. No account needed to play locally; your OGS login uses OAuth and Hoshi never stores your password.

  • 文

    English and SpanishNew

    The whole app switches language live from Preferences. English by default.

  • ⚙

    Play against any engineNew

    Hoshi's own KataGo is ready out of the box; add Leela Zero, GNU Go, Pachi or any GTP engine with its command line. Choose colours, board, handicap, komi and rules with Ctrl+G.

  • ⇋

    Engine vs engineNew

    Let two engines play each other and watch the game grow in the tree. Pause, go back to any move and try your own idea: the engine answers from there.

  • ⌨

    GTP consoleNew

    See every command and answer between Hoshi and its engines, read their logs and type GTP commands yourself.

  • ◎

    Analysis with your engineNew

    Any engine with lz-analyze or kata-analyze can drive the analysis panel instead of the built-in KataGo. All engines stay off during your OGS games.

  • ✦

    Moves you can feel

    The move that wins a fight shakes the board, cracks the wood and throws embers; routine opening moves just click. Captured stones shatter and weak groups glow faintly. Too much? Turn it down to subtle or off at any time with F.

  • ♪

    A calm lo-fi loop that adds drums, arpeggios and an overdrive layer as strong moves at key moments stack up. It stays calm through the opening and cools down when you slip.

  • ☾

    Themes, boards and stonesNew

    Five themes — Night sky, Ink and gold, Zen garden, Warm minimal and a Classic tribute — plus six gobans, five stone sets and ten backgrounds to mix with any of them.

Review

Study a game the way a teacher would.

Pin what mattered, draw what you saw, share it with a friend, and test yourself on it later. Everything stays inside the game's SGF file.

01Pin it

S opens the study, or the Study button under the board. Pick a category, add a note and press Pin. Under “Sign as” you choose the name your notes carry.

02Draw it

Drag for arrows and areas; click for circles and letters; the eraser removes a drawing. Five colours. Drawings belong to the move and never change the game.

03What if…

Lay out a numbered line of stones without playing it; click the last stone to take it back. On a move pinned as a mistake the line starts with the better move, and that becomes the quiz's answer.

04Share it

“Save study…” writes one SGF with the game and every note. Your friend opens it with “Open study…”, replies and sends it back: Hoshi merges it move by move without duplicates. Sabaki and other programs show it as comments.

05Quiz and report

Mistakes, good moves, key moments, joseki and life-and-death pins make the questions. “Report…” saves a printable page with every moment on a small board, the notes and KataGo's verdict. See a sample report

06Fair on OGS

During your OGS games you can still take notes and draw, but there is no engine and no quiz, and a drawing tool never sends a move. Your notes stay with the game in your library.

Joseki

Never forget a joseki again.

Study the standard corner sequences while you play, then drill them until they stick.

01Spaced repetition

Six Leitner boxes, from ten minutes to a month. A perfect run waits longer; a mistake comes back soon.

02Pick what to study

Filter by 4-4, 3-4, 3-3, 4-5 or 3-5, choose a line from the list, or let Hoshi pick what is due.

03Endless lines

“New line from OGS” follows only ideal and good moves of the Joseki Explorer. Import SGF collections or record lines from your own board.

Music

A soundtrack that plays along.

Six layers composed in code join one by one as you string good moves together. A blunder stops the tape. Time cools it back down.

  • Warm electric piano and vinyl crackle
  • Bass and a soft kick
  • Swung drums
  • Plucked arpeggio with echo
  • Pumping chords, claps and a bright lead
  • Overdrive: four-on-the-floor, rolling bass, fills

Demo: calm, then a streak of strong moves from 0:16 and a blunder at 1:10… you'll hear it.

正

Fair play built in

During live games on OGS, KataGo stays off — OGS forbids AI help while you play. The joseki hints and the trainer are off too. The effects and the music follow the fight on the board instead (contact, liberties, captures), and Hoshi's own quick territory estimate is there. Every game is kept in your library, where the AI review — with all its effects — is waiting after the game.

Get Hoshi

Download and play.

Installers for Windows, macOS and Linux, built with Avalonia. Nothing else to install: .NET comes inside. Once installed, Hoshi updates itself in one click and only downloads what changed.

Download

Free · open source · other systems below

Windows

Windows 10 or 11. Run the installer: Hoshi appears in the Start menu and on the desktop, and updates itself.

x64ARM64

First launch: if SmartScreen warns about an unknown publisher, choose “More info” → “Run anyway”. Portable: x64 · ARM64

macOS

macOS 12 or later. Open the .pkg and follow the steps: Hoshi goes to Applications and updates itself.

Apple SiliconIntel

Hoshi is not notarized by Apple yet: if macOS refuses the .pkg, right-click it → Open, or allow it in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Portable: Apple Silicon · Intel

Linux

Any recent x64 or ARM64 distribution. One file: make it executable and run it — it updates itself.

x64ARM64
chmod +x HoshiGo-linux-x64.AppImage
./HoshiGo-linux-x64.AppImage

If it does not start, install FUSE 2 (sudo apt install libfuse2). Music needs PulseAudio/PipeWire (pacat) or ALSA (aplay). Classic archive: x64 · ARM64

KataGo included

The Windows and Linux downloads come with KataGo and a quick network, ready from the first start. On a Mac, run brew install katago and Hoshi finds it. Stronger networks can be picked in Preferences → Analysis.

Build from source

With the .NET 10 SDK:

git clone https://github.com/BuddhaCodes/BadukDana.git
cd BadukDana
dotnet run --project src/Hoshi.App -c Release
